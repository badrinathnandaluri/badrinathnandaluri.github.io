Privacy policy

clipper, the personal publishing tool of the bootstraped_07 channel. Effective 21 November 2026.

Who this applies to

clipper is used only by the owner of the bootstraped_07 YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel (youtube.upload scope), granted by the owner through Google's sign-in.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the owner's own professional account, granted by the owner through Instagram's sign-in.

clipper does not read, collect or store any other personal data, and never accesses anyone else's account.

Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than Google and Meta, which receive them to carry out the uploads. There are no cookies, analytics or ads.

Third-party services

clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Instagram publishing follows Meta's Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions and in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Removing access stops all uploads immediately.

Contact

Questions: through the channel at youtube.com/@bootstraped_07.
